Core platform and broader scope

MODU develops a deterministic, local-first AI and device-orchestration platform for coordinating auditable agents, tools, models, computers, phones, and connected services. P-Core and the surrounding execution/control architecture remain part of that core direction.

The company is also developing additive programs in applied AI, sovereign/private systems, drones and autonomy, mobile/device systems, RF and resilient operations, cybersecurity, industrial automation, manufacturing/test systems, and defence/public-sector R&D and integration.

This is expansion, not a rebrand. A new program does not replace MODU's core mission or turn the company into a single-purpose defence, cloud, drone, or cybersecurity business. Each program carries its own maturity, regulatory, evidence, and partner boundaries.
