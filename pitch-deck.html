Problem

Today’s AI is powerful, but operationally shallow.

High recurring cost

Useful workloads are pushed toward metered cloud inference, even when capable local hardware is idle.

Fragmented tools

Agents, scripts, bridges, plugins, and devices behave like separate products instead of one coherent execution system.

Weak verification

Most assistants can claim an action succeeded without binding the response to the actual worker, command, page, output, and receipt.

Lost context

Knowledge, permissions, procedures, and device capabilities are repeatedly rediscovered rather than reused as durable infrastructure.

Solution

One runtime, one identity, many execution surfaces.

Procedural cognitive OS

A structured runtime combines deterministic procedures, memory, tool selection, knowledge graphs, permissions, and LLM reasoning.

Local and heterogeneous compute

MODU uses the owner’s PC GPU, CPU, phones, local AI accelerators, and cloud resources as one governed worker fabric.

Result-bound execution

Actions are tied to operation IDs, adapters, workers, page and command digests, stdout/stderr, receipts, and final user-visible results.

Resumable human gates

CAPTCHA, MFA, passkeys, legal terms, and payments pause a workflow without losing the exact page, session, pending action, or evidence.

Technical differentiation

LLMs become a reasoning component—not the entire product.

  • Deterministic execution layer: procedures define what must happen, what evidence is required, and what counts as success.
  • Canonical company and user context: identities, permissions, devices, resources, and programme states are resolved once and reused.
  • Adapter and bridge federation: connected apps are used first; local/browser bridges handle what connectors cannot.
  • Failure recovery: workflows resume from durable state rather than restarting applications, submissions, or device jobs.
  • Cost-aware scheduling: local compute is preferred where it produces the required quality; cloud resources become selective accelerators.
