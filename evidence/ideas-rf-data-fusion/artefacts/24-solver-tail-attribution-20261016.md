## 1. Headline

The tail is not in the sky, the site, or the phone's measurement path. **≈95% of it is the solver's own arithmetic:** the solver estimates one bias *in Hz* pooled across every carrier in the epoch — 1176.45 MHz (L5/E5-class) through 1604.81 MHz (GLONASS) — during a 0.82 ppm clock episode. A true clock drift is a *fractional* offset: at 1176.45 MHz the same drift is 960 Hz, not 1,286 Hz, so every L5-class measurement keeps a ≈ **−326 Hz** residual after the pooled subtraction. Re-running the *unmodified* solver on frequency-scaled residuals (the counterfactual a ppm-domain estimator implements) collapses the pooled corrected p90 on the identical data from **340.2 Hz to 15.8 Hz** (median 17.4 → 7.8 Hz). The three "elevated" satellites file `23` left unexplained are ordinary satellites whose pooled medians were composition artefacts of their L5 measurement share (§2). TLE/SGP4 prediction error, priced against IGS precise products, is ≈4 Hz of the small remainder — real, and worth having, but it was never the tail.

## 4. Kill-criteria verdicts

- **K1 (tail is a floor) — NOT triggered.** The precise arm cuts the *pooled* p90 by only 0.5% (<10% clause met at pooled level), but K1 also requires all three signature fits to be null; they are not (carrier structure r = 0.998; arc smoothness 0.357 vs null p95 0.016; Doppler-rate ρ = −0.286, p ≈ 4×10⁻⁹⁹; the age fit alone is null, and the elevation fit is significant but orbit-explained). The tail is structured, named, and mostly self-inflicted — the opposite of an unstructured noise floor. The p90 ≈ 340 Hz / RMS ≈ 200 Hz figures must **not** be recorded as design constants.
- **K2 (no noise-chasing per-SV fixes) — the per-SV offset fix FAILS as specified, and is killed for the as-built series.** Offsets fit on epochs 1–133, applied to 134–266 of the pooled corrected series: held-out p90 323.6 → 313.1 Hz = **3.2% cut** (< 30% bar), despite sign consistency of 9/9 large-offset SVs — the offsets encode each SV's carrier mix, not per-SV truth, and a scalar per SV cannot represent a per-carrier term. Diagnostics (reported as diagnostics, not as a K2 pass): the identical estimator on the carrier-scaled counterfactual series cuts held-out p90 15.6 → 2.8 Hz (82.4%), and on the L1 stratum alone 18.5 → 2.8 Hz (84.9%) — per-SV offsets are stable and predictive *within this 4.4-minute arc* once the artefact is removed by construction. Persistence across days/thermal states is unmeasured; any future per-SV stage must re-pass K2 on the carrier-scaled series across sessions before it is built.
- **K3 (no synthetic substitution) — honoured.** Precise products were obtained for GPS only (IGS rapid; §6). Galileo, BeiDou, and GLONASS prediction shares remain **unresolved** — no MGEX product for 2026-10-12 was reachable from the estate's networks on 2026-10-16 (BKG's MGEX mirror ends at week 2237; IGN / GFZ / CODE / WHU / SOPAC connections timed out; CDDIS is Earthdata-login-gated; JPL's multi-GNSS rapid area ends in 2019 and its finals for week 2440 were not yet posted). No synthetic data was substituted anywhere in this pass.

## 5. Clock note (the 266 bias estimates as an Allan dataset) + correction to file 23

**Allan deviation of the per-epoch bias series** (266 estimates, Δt ≈ 1.0 s): ADEV rises monotonically — 2.68 Hz @ 1 epoch, 4.49 @ 2, 7.13 @ 4, 11.18 @ 8, 17.17 @ 16, 25.64 @ 32, 34.1 Hz @ 64 — log-log slope (1→8 epochs) **+0.69**, steeper than random-walk FM (+0.5): over this arc the clock *wanders smoothly* (bias IQR 102.6 Hz) rather than jittering. ADEV(1 epoch) = 2.7 Hz is consistent with pure estimation noise at ~43 SVs/epoch (per-SV scatter ≈13 Hz / √43 ≈ 2 Hz), so in rich epochs per-epoch independent estimation costs almost nothing — the median result stands. The production case for a constrained clock tracker is the *thin* epochs (4–8 SVs, catalogue edge cases), where the same scatter gives a 5–7 Hz estimation error and a tracker with Allan-shaped process noise (random-walk class, scale set by the slope above) would beat a fresh solve. One paragraph, as commissioned; no solver change is proposed by it.

**Correction to file `23`, §6 limit 4.** That limit bounds the Hz-pooling remainder at "≤ ~35 Hz worst case at this drift." The bound holds only *within* the L1 band (carriers 1561–1605 MHz). Across the capture's full carrier set — 37.7% of measurements at 1176.45 MHz — the measured remainder is **≈326 Hz** in this episode (§3, row 1). File `23`'s headline delta (median 1,221 → 17 Hz; 266/266 epoch flips) is unaffected: the median is L1-dominated and the flips are verdicts on epoch RMS against a 500 Hz tier. Its corrected p90 and epoch-RMS figures should be read with this annex attached.

**Updated bounded claim wording this evidence supports** (for the design sessions / Oct 22 to adopt or not): *"In a recorded 266-epoch GNSS capture (11,049 measurements, 2026-10-12) carrying a ≈0.82 ppm receiver clock bias, the clock-bias solver's corrected p90 of 340 Hz decomposes as ≈324 Hz of estimator construction (one bias estimated in Hz, pooled across carriers 1176–1605 MHz), ≈4 Hz of TLE/SGP4 prediction error at L1 (GPS stratum, against IGS rapid precise products), an epoch-timing term of ≈1.2 s × Doppler rate in the measurement timestamps, and a per-stratum measurement floor of ≈2 Hz p90. Re-estimating the same solver's bias carrier-scaled (ppm-domain) on the same data yields a pooled corrected p90 of 15.8 Hz."* It does **not** support: a typical-epoch figure (this remains a bias-episode capture, file `23` §5), a geolocation figure, or a per-SV-offset fix (K2).

**The design question this prices** (the enrichment read's closing question — which input to upgrade, and what each buys, in Hz): the estimator's domain (Hz → carrier-scaled) buys ≈324 Hz of p90 in bias episodes and is a solver-internal change; precise ephemeris buys ≈4 Hz at stratum p90 and is a data-input change; timestamp alignment buys the Doppler-rate-structured term (typically 3–13 Hz at L1) and is a collector change; per-SV state buys nothing licensed (K2) until it re-passes across sessions on the corrected series.

- **Dataset identity.** Same capture as file `23` §2 (Legion `/home/jason/phone-mine/p9_pull_20261012/phonelab-captures/20261012T194749Z/`, both GNSS jsonl files), rebuilt with file `23`'s exact parse, 250 ms epoch clustering, mapping, elevation mask, and solver call. - **Estate code.** `modu-rf-lab` @ `44c31c71` (branch `wren/ghost-journal-changeonly-20261012`), solver `clock_correct.py` unmodified in every arm. The precise arm is a *new variant script* kept outside the repo (`precise_arm.py` in the artefacts): it mirrors `doppler.py`'s frame math line-for-line (pipeline GMST, ECI range-rate incl. Earth-rotation observer velocity) with the state source swapped. The canonical solver and the catalogue were not touched; all runs read-only against inputs, detached (systemd user units) on the Legion.
- **Precise products.** IGS rapid (rapid, not final — finals for 2026-10-12 do not exist yet): `IGS0OPSRAP_20262850000_01D_15M_ORB.SP3.gz` (sha256 `23208188…`) + `IGS0OPSRAP_20262850000_01D_05M_CLK.CLK.gz` (sha256 `3011c561…`), fetched 2026-10-16 from the BKG mirror (`igs.bkg.bund.de/root_ftp/IGS/products/2440/`). GPS week 2440, DOY 285. Both products are **GPS-only** (SP3 header lists 32 G satellites; CLK AS records all G) — coverage 3,979 / 3,979 GPS measurements, all 9 GPS SVs, both carriers. Orbits: 11-node barycentric Lagrange interpolation of SP3 ECEF positions with analytic derivative for velocity, rotated to ECI by the pipeline's GMST convention with the ω × r transport term; SV clock rate from a quadratic fit to CLK offsets (±2 epochs), entering the prediction as +f × δ̇_sv. Products are in GPS time, evaluated at UTC + 18 s. Elevation/mask inherited from the TLE arm (identical measurement set in both arms). Self-check: GPS L1 median residual falls to 4.0 Hz and every single-carrier GPS SV improves or holds — a frame or time-base error would present at 50–500 Hz, not 3–6 Hz.
- **Counterfactual (row 1).** `correct_epoch()` run on residuals scaled by F₀/fᵢ (F₀ = 1575.42 MHz), results mapped back by fᵢ/F₀ — the estimator a ppm-domain solver implements, using the unmodified solver as the engine. Labelled a diagnostic decomposition wherever it appears; it is not a landed solver variant.
- **Signature fits.** On the TLE-arm corrected series: (a) per-SV L1-band median |residual| vs TLE age (Spearman); (b) lag-1 autocorrelation of per-SV signed series over consecutive epochs, Fisher-z pooled, against a 200× epoch-shuffle null; (c) Spearman of signed residual vs predicted Doppler rate (centred finite difference of the prediction series) and of |residual| vs elevation, with binned medians. Pooled-series fits are reported only where the carrier artefact cannot manufacture the result; stratified fits are primary (§3).
- **K2 protocol (as written).** Per-SV offsets = median signed corrected residual over epochs 1–133 (indices 0–132); applied unchanged to epochs 134–266; pass requires ≥30% cut in held-out corrected p90 **and** sign-consistent per-SV estimates (checked on SVs with |offset| ≥ 20 Hz: 9/9 agree — agreement does not rescue the failed cut). Stratified variants (L1-only; carrier-scaled series) run identically and are labelled diagnostics.
- **TDCP arm (measurement path).** The capture carries ADR (`adr_m`, `adr_state`, `adr_uncertainty_m` on every raw record). Method: consecutive same-(SV, constellation, carrier) ADR differences over 0.5–2.0 s intervals (13,282 candidates; 368 excluded for RESET/CYCLE_SLIP flags — 2.8%, i.e. no duty-cycling massacre on this chipset; 12,901 sane; 10,616 joined to usable residuals); ADR↔PRR median correlation 0.955 (sign convention calibrated against PRR, reported as a convention check). Result: the PR-minus-TDCP disagreement (median |d| = 28.8 Hz, p90 166.5 Hz; GPS-L1-only 36.8 Hz; quality-gated subset — ADR uncertainty < 0.1 m and C/N0 > 35 dB-Hz — 36.3 Hz; only 25.8% of intervals agree within 10 Hz) exceeds the entire PR residual floor it was meant to price, and TDCP residuals solver-corrected per epoch scatter at median 152 Hz vs 17 Hz for the matched PR residuals. Verdict: **this device's recorded ADR stream is not a usable TDCP reference at Hz precision** (candidate causes — chipset ADR synthesis/smoothing inconsistent with the 1 s batch cadence, unflagged half-cycle events — are named as candidates, not established). The measurement share is therefore bounded by the precise-arm floor (§3, row 4) and reported as unseparated beyond that bound, per the proposal's own fallback rule. The sidereal discriminator was not run: ADR existed (the arm ran), and independently no sidereal-matched same-site capture exists to run it against.
