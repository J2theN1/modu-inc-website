Published by MODU INDUSTRIES LTD. (BC1549916), West Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada. Proposal to the IDEaS RF Data Fusion for Command and Control challenge (CFP 006, solicitation W7714-248676/0NN), Component 1b — Design.

MODU INDUSTRIES LTD. is a British Columbia company, solely owned and directed by Jason Martin Vetter, working from its laboratory in West Kelowna, BC. All labour on this proposal is performed in Canada.

A distinction held throughout the proposal and on this page: the platform is TRL 6; the proposed solution is claimed at TRL 4. The evidence below supports the TRL 4 claim — components built and validated in MODU's laboratory — and no higher claim is made anywhere on this page.

The proposal

The proposed solution is the Sovereign Fusion Layer (SFL): bearer-agnostic middleware that ingests domain data arriving over tactical RF bearers, normalizes it into a canonical, standards-aligned information model, fuses and prioritizes it, and emits and consumes structured exchanges in MTF-XML, NIEM and NCDF formats — for operation in denied, degraded, intermittent and low-bandwidth (DDIL) conditions. SFL does not yet exist as an integrated system; its components do. Component 1b designs the integrated system and demonstrates an integrated prototype in a simulated bearer environment (exit TRL 5). Total price: CAD $1,075,000 (Component 1b cap $1,500,000; profit cap $161,250).

1. Receiver clock-bias solver — measured on recorded data. On the retained 2026-10-12 GNSS capture (266 epochs, 11,049 measurements), the solver reduced the pooled median absolute Doppler residual from 1,221.3 Hz to 17.4 Hz. A companion record attributes the corrected p90 tail (≈340 Hz) to estimator construction (≈324 Hz), prediction and timing terms, and a ≈2 Hz floor; a fractional-domain (ppm) estimator variant was prototyped and validated on held-out data (p90 15.6 Hz; both pre-stated kill criteria passed; guard floor 454 Hz stated). Artefacts: three measurement records, held here with SHA-256 hashes (pack files 23, 24, 26). 2. Receive-only RF ingest, by construction. MODU's RF practice is receive-side. The ingest discipline runs on a receive-only RF broker (the receive-only RF broker specified by its manifest — 10 allowlisted receive-only operations, no transmit primitive by construction; 41 of 41 checks — re-created harness run, 2026-10-17; version 1 built 2026-10-20 compiles in the six read operations and is device-proven — see below) and raw GNSS/sensor pipelines (9,256 raw GNSS measurements in a single quality-gated capture).

On 2026-10-20 the broker itself was built and proven on the device. Version 1 is a one-shot tool — invoked per call under root, nothing resident, no listening socket — compiling in exactly six of the manifest's ten allowlisted operations, all of them reads (get_magic, get_version, get_channel, get_radio, get_promisc, get_monitor). The manifest's three SET operations and its sample operation are not compiled in at all: they exist in the source as refused names only, so the no-transmit property is a property of the binary, not of a setting. Every call passes a canary discipline (the magic/version pair is re-read before any other operation is issued; on mismatch or failure the operation is refused and the refusal recorded). On the live device, under restored root, all six reads executed and answered — the channel read back as 44, agreeing at the same minute with the live association's 5220 MHz — and a hostile-name batch (SET names, raw opcode numbers, path-shaped names) was refused 8 of 8 before any driver call was made. The broker ships as a Magisk module (modu-bcm4390-rx-broker), installed with its rollback rehearsed (remove + reinstall from the same package, state verified at each step). The ZSAT fabric's registry validates the backend live, from measured values — evidence state LIVE_BEHAVIOR_MATCH, registry read 2026-10-20T10:22:13Z carrying magic 0x14e46c77, version 2, channel 44, radio 0, promisc 0, monitor 0. Host test suite for the broker: 5 of 5, plus the branch's manifest/probe tests 2 of 2; the branch's full suite ran 445 passed with 2 failures reproduced identically on the pre-build tree (environmental, not regressions). Artefacts: the broker manifest (config/zsat/pixel9_bcm4390_rx_broker.json — the ten allowlisted operations as configured) and its test suite (tests/test_zsat_pixel9_bcm4390_rx_broker.py), held in the estate's RF convergence worktree and located by the 2026-10-20 RF census (~/workspace/wren/rf-estate-census-20261020.md §3); the BCM4390 read-only probe record (estate record ~/workspace/bcm4390-probes-20261009.md) — the same receive-only discipline proven on-device 2026-10-09 (WLC_GET_ reads only; no SET opcodes in the probe manifest) and re-proven 2026-10-20 under restored root, when the probe's two reads returned their first privileged answers (WLC_GET_MAGIC and WLC_GET_VERSION, both rc=0; the stock runs of August 2026 had returned errno 13; probe source audited read-only — estate record ~/workspace/wren/rf-deep-branch-characterization-20261020.md, Step-3 addendum); the broker source (scripts/modu_bcm4390_rx_broker.c) and its unit-test output — landed early, 2026-10-20, on branch wren/p9-dongle-broker-20261020 in the estate's RF repository, with its build record (~/workspace/wren/p9-dongle-build-20261020.md); the GNSS capture manifest — added 2026-10-22. That is the engineering pattern the proposed solution generalizes from the space domain to message standards. Live read (2026-10-21): the catalogue is flowing. Read live from the production store on 2026-10-21 (07:03 PDT), it holds 940,848 observations over 103 GNSS satellites (GPS 32 · GLONASS 24 · BeiDou 31 · Galileo 16), spanning 2026-10-10T03:03:49Z → 2026-10-21T14:00:20Z. 10. NTN modem reverse engineering — the satellite bearer, taken apart offline. MODU has reverse-engineered the Pixel 9's Shannon 5400 NTN modem firmware (94 MB image, build g5400e-250605) entirely offline: the image's section table parsed (BOOT / PSP / MAIN / APM / VSS / DBGCORE / INFO present; the RF-configuration and NV stores proven to load separately and absent from the image), the NTN band tables (N255 / N256) extracted exactly as tabled against 3GPP, 15 modem IPC commands and the AT command set mapped (+NTNSATCFG, +NTNMETRIC, +NTNTEST, +SKYLOTEST among them), the Skylo satellite-service feature gates and NV items named, and the S5511 RF front end (MIPI RFFE) mapped. The same work established where the satellite-service access gate sits: no "731" exists on the modem side — the gate is on the application-processor side — the finding the ground-side instrumentation cited at claim 2 is built on. That instrumentation has since made the one live contact this analysis pointed to: on 2026-10-20 its injection module was verified live inside the satellite service process, and its single read-only transaction (code 31, requestIsRilConnected) executed on the device and answered true. The answer is a service-level RIL-connection state, returned from the service's own cached state; the proof client of that pass could express no other transaction, and nothing was transmitted (claim 2; execution record ~/workspace/wren/p9-root-restore-execution-20261020.md, FLIGHT 7). As far as the modem is concerned this remains completed offline analysis, evidenced in retained records: no modem function was invoked by the reverse engineering.

The same instrumentation has since reached the modem itself — at the request layer, and only there. On 2026-10-20, after source-level characterization established that creating the satellite service provider is construction plus inert arming only (no RIL request, no registration, no callback), the client created the provider (ISatellite code 1) and — inside a single held binding, the provider being torn down when its creator unbinds — issued the one-shot modem-metric request (code 22) twice, as (true, 1) and (false, 1). Both requests executed the full chain to the modem: RIL request 731 was issued (clientId 8, serials 3 and 4, slot 0) and answered by the modem in 58 ms and 52 ms respectively, each with the bare completion event and no payload. The request layer is therefore proven end-to-end, including the modem's own answer. Across the 100-second observation window the modem returned no metric data — zero 9706 indications — and none was expected: no NTN session exists on this device to generate metric events, so the metric-data leg is unproven and is not claimed. The request's argument semantics were narrowed by the two calls' symmetric behaviour toward the framework's (reset, transactionId) reading; that remains an inference, stated as one. Nothing was transmitted over any bearer, and no NTN link is established or claimed (execution record ~/workspace/wren/p9-root-restore-execution-20261020.md, FLIGHT 9). Artefacts: the modem RE record (estate record ~/workspace/lane2-modem-ntn-20261010.md) and its companion analyses (Legion pixel-9-lab records of 2026-10-09/10: modem firmware, RF floor truth, BCM4390 emulation, BCM4390 HCI RE, AOSP antenna/RF); the RF front-end map (config/zsat/pixel9_rf_frontend_map.json, estate RF convergence worktree); estate RF census of 2026-10-20 (~/workspace/wren/rf-estate-census-20261020.md §3); the root-restore and crossing execution record of 2026-10-20 (~/workspace/wren/p9-root-restore-execution-20261020.md, FLIGHT 7) for the executed transaction; the root-restore and crossing execution record of 2026-10-20, FLIGHT 9 (same file) for the provider creation and the modem-answered metric pair.

What is not claimed

  • No HF work exists in this estate and none is claimed. MODU's RF practice is receive-side satellite work; the proposal claims measurement discipline, not HF measurement practice.
  • No MTF-XML, NIEM or NCDF message set has been implemented yet; no integrated fusion prototype exists; no component has been tested on a live tactical bearer. Those are precisely the Component 1b design tasks.
  • The catalogue figure is re-read live at integration and on submission day and quoted with its read date (see claim 3). No figure on this page is carried forward from an earlier draft without a dated read behind it.
  • The published catalogue snapshot (claim 3) is a frozen reference dated 2026-10-10 → 2026-10-17; it evidences the catalogue capability and is never quoted as the submission's catalogue figure.
  • No operational NTN link is claimed. The modem evidence (claim 10) is offline reverse engineering of a firmware image; MODU's satellite instrumentation is ground-side and receive-only. The single executed satellite-service transaction (claims 2 and 10) is application-processor IPC answered from the service's cached state — it is not bearer traffic and establishes no link. No traffic has been sent or received over an NTN bearer, and no RF has been transmitted in any of the work evidenced on this page.
  • The binder instrumentation is proven to the extent executed, and no further. On 2026-10-20 the satellite-service instrumentation executed: one read-only state query (code 31, answered true from the service's cached state); one provider construction (code 1, characterized beforehand as construction and inert arming only); and two one-shot modem-metric requests (code 22) that reached the modem and were answered by bare acknowledgement with no data. No metric data has ever been received, no other satellite-service behaviour is evidenced, and none of it is bearer traffic or an NTN capability (execution record ~/workspace/wren/p9-root-restore-execution-20261020.md, FLIGHTS 7 and 9).
  • The on-device AI evidence (claims 5 substrate, 11, 12) is single-session laboratory measurement on MODU's own stack. No device-vendor AI service is used or claimed for any capability on this page; the generation and embedding evidenced here run on MODU's own model files through MODU's own serving stack. The job daemon's persistence across device restarts is unproven, its model selection is not pinned (the completion at claim 11 was served by the model named in its record, which is not the registry's default), and its retrieval-search job type was down during the 2026-10-24 session and is not evidenced by that session.
  • The below-HAL evidence (claim 12) is interface recovery plus two read-only executions, and only that. The 48-byte command payload format inside the driver's mailbox structure remains unrecovered; no below-HAL command was submitted to the coprocessor in any work evidenced on this page, and no accelerator workload is claimed to run through that interface. (The accelerator workloads evidenced on this page run through the device's standard neural-network delegate — claims 5 and 11.)
